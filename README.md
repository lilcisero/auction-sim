# Auction Sim

Fantasy basketball auction simulator for 14-team, $200, 9-category head-to-head leagues.
It simulates thousands of auctions and seasons to find which draft strategy wins the most
titles, gives you a max bid for every player, and tracks your live auction.

**Use it:** https://lilcisero.github.io/auction-sim/

Everything runs in your browser; your runs and draft are saved on your own device.
Stats: last NBA season's game logs via stats.nba.com.
